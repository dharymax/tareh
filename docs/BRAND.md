# TAREH Brand Assets

Official product name: طارح  
Latin name: TAREH  
Tagline: كرة القدم في متناولك

## Logo
- Primary source asset: assets/brand/tareh-logo.png
- Preserve original proportions and transparency.
- Use contain fit; do not crop the crown or Arabic wordmark.
- Provide dark and light backgrounds with sufficient contrast.

## Visual direction
- Premium dark interface.
- Navy/near-black surfaces.
- Gold as a restrained accent matching the supplied crown/wordmark.
- Clear white text and accessible contrast.
- Arabic RTL layout and legible Arabic typography.

## App icon
Use assets/brand/tareh-icon-256.png as the repository launcher icon. The optimized 256 × 256 PNG preserves transparency and the supplied mark. For Android launcher assets, generate adaptive icon foreground/background variants and test safe-zone cropping on multiple launchers. Do not stretch the logo.

## Usage
Do not recolor, distort or redraw the supplied logo without approval.
